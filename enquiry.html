What wedding families usually ask

Common destination wedding discussions before a venue is shortlisted.

Most serious wedding enquiries are not just about price. They are about whether the venue can carry the scale, style, hospitality, and guest experience the family expects across multiple functions.

Ceremony and reception flow

Mandap placement, bridal entry, stage visibility, dining movement, and guest circulation.

Decor and wedding mood

Theme direction, floral design, mehendi styling, reception lighting, and night ambience.

Hospitality and dining flow

Buffet arrangement, live counters, family service zones, dessert islands, and guest comfort.

Arrival and guest handling

Airport or city arrivals, valet, room check-ins, power backup, and event-day coordination.

Rooms and family stays

Wedding family accommodation, outstation guest rooms, and AC or Non AC, Standard or Deluxe stay preferences.
